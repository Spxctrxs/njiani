*Njiani — Every Journey Starts Here.*

Njiani is an intelligent, offline-first mobility platform designed to simplify urban transportation in Kenya, starting with Nairobi. It helps commuters discover matatu stages, find and compare routes, estimate fares and travel times, and access transport information even without internet. The platform also integrates an AI mobility assistant, community traffic updates, and a car hire marketplace, bringing essential mobility services together in one place.

*Our mission:* To make urban mobility smarter, more accessible, and more reliable for everyone.
